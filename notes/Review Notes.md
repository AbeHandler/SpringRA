# Attention Review

Created: January 8, 2026 9:09 PM

### Reason to Disregard (Out of Scope)

- Attention mechanisms in large language models (e.g., BERT Transformers)
- Attention disorders
- General linguistic usage (e.g., lack of attention to something / pay attention to something)

### Major Themes:

- ***Investor Attention***
    
    Measurements:
    
    - search-based measures of investor attention (e.g., google search index, co-search behavior like the search spillover from the focal firm to its suppliers)
    
    Mechanisms:
    
    From my understanding, “attention” functions as a latent mechanism. Below can draw investors’ attention, and then in turn alter their further investing behavior and decision-making. (stimulus - attention - behavior)
    
    - visibility on social platforms
    - advertising appearance
    - information disclosure (e.g., cash flow disclosure, earnings announcement)
    - voluntary disclosure
    - International political events
- ***Stakeholder Attention***
    
    Whom:
    
    - Stakeholders refer to entities that have shared interests with the focal firm (e.g., investors, financial analysts, suppliers, alliances)
    
    Mechanisms:
    
    - Signals from the focal company will change stakeholders’ behavior (e.g., a strong earnings announcement by the focal firm can make alliance partners more willing to collaborate)
- ***Consumer Attention***
    
    For consumer attention, I feel it is different from investor attention. Most of the literature uses consumer attention as a dependent variable (DV).
    
    Measurements:
    
    - Plenty of papers use eye-tracking to measure consumers’ attention
    
    Mechanisms:
    
    Below can attract consumers’ attention, thus facilitating purchase behavior.
    
    - advertising design (e.g., layout, content)
    - location-based advertising
    - background music
    - product price / risk
    - AI can bias consumer attention
- ***Manager Attention***
    
    Whom:
    
    - Most of studies focus on CEOs
    
    Mechanisms:
    
    Managers have limited attention, and what they focus on influences the firm’s strategic priorities, in terms of the following aspects:
    
    - firm decision-making
    - firm performance
    - corporate social responsibility; environmental, social, and governance (ESG)
- ***Organization Attention***
    
    Similar to the manager attention, based on the attention-based view of the firm, a firm’s development is guided by its attention. External information attracts firms’ attention and influences their actions.
    
    - pay attention to emergent social issues
    - response to media pressure
    - take care of gender diversity
- ***Cognitive Attention***
    
    There is a small body of literature on cognitive attention. These studies identify general patterns that do not target a specific audience but instead apply broadly across individuals.
    
    Such as, how people pay attention to the following:
    
    - stimulus (e.g., negative feedback, threat)
    - facial expression (e.g., fearful expression, eye-size)
- ***Employee Attention***
    
    How to better help employees focus in order to improve work efficiency.
    
    - music background
- ***Platform User Attention***
    
    The literature on platform user attention mainly approaches the topic from two perspectives.
    
    From the perspective of users:
    
    - how can users seek attention from other users (e.g., being an online hater)
    
    From the perspective of platform governance:
    
    - for example, how to balance user limited attention between addictive content (interesting but no knowledge) and high quality content (learn knowledge but boring)
- ***Product User Attention***
    
    How product design features or flaws influence users’ attention during the usage process？
    
    - IT product (e.g., system interruption)
- ***Public Attention***
    
    How public attention forces firms to take social responsibility and enables more effective government regulation.
    
    - media pressure
    - effective regulation

### Related Theory:

- ***Signaling Theory***
    - limited attention paid to signals
- ***Cognitive Niches View***
    - explain selective attention
- ***Spillover Effects***
    - attention spillover to related products / topics / assets
